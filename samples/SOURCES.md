# Sample sources and rights

All three MusicXML files in this directory were created for this repository on
8 October 2026. They are short, original synthetic teaching examples rather
than transcriptions of archival or published compositions:

- `stepwise-study.musicxml` — a C-major ascending scale in 4/4.
- `waltz-arpeggio.musicxml` — a G-major arpeggio pattern in 3/4 with eighth and
  quarter notes.
- `low-register-study.musicxml` — a short F-major-pattern exercise in 2/4.

They are distributed under the repository's Apache 2.0 license. Titles and the
composer label “Music Score Analysis Explorer” identify their demonstrative
origin. Their purpose is to provide known inputs for testing analysis; they
must not be described as collection material, historical sources, or evidence
of OMR performance.
